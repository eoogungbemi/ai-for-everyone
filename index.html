The basics

What is AI, really?

No robots taking over. Here's what AI actually is, in plain English.

🧠

What it is

AI is computer software that learns from huge numbers of examples. It spots patterns and makes very good guesses, but it doesn't think or feel like a person.

📱

Where you meet it

Predictive text, voice assistants, spam filters, sat-nav routes, "you might also like" suggestions and chatbots all use AI.

✅

What it's good at

Quick, repetitive jobs like summarising text, translating, drafting emails, sorting photos and answering everyday questions.

⚠️

Where it goes wrong

It can state wrong facts confidently, repeat unfair biases from its training examples, and be used to make convincing fakes.

    Good habits

    5 simple ways to stay safe with AI

    You don't need to be an expert. These habits go a long way.

    • 🔍

      Double-check the facts

      AI can sound sure of itself and still be wrong. For anything important (health, money, the law), check a trusted source.

    • 🔒

      Keep personal info private

      Never type passwords, bank details, ID numbers or private family information into a chatbot.

    • 🎭

      Question shocking videos and voices

      AI can fake faces and voices. If a clip or phone call seems surprising or urgent, pause and check before you believe it or share it.

    • ⚖️

      Remember AI can be unfair

      AI learns from human examples, including our biases. Be wary when it's used to judge people, such as in job or loan decisions.

    • 🙋

      You're in charge

      Treat AI as a helpful assistant, not the boss. You make the final decision.
